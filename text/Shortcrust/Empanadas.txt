Name:
Empanadas
Information:
The empanadas are usually assembled by placing a small amount of filling in the center of a circle of dough, folding the dough over to create a half-moon shape, and crimping the edges to seal the filling inside. 
They are then baked or fried until golden and crispy.
Empanadas can be enjoyed as a snack, appetizer, or main course, and are often served with a side of salsa, guacamole, or hot sauce for dipping. 
They are a popular street food in many Latin American countries and are also commonly served at parties, festivals, and other social gatherings.
Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cubed
1/4 to 1/2 cup ice water
1 lb ground beef
1 onion, diced
1 red bell pepper, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp ground cumin
1/2 tsp paprika
1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
Salt and pepper, to taste
1/2 cup pitted green olives, sliced
2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
1 egg, beaten for egg wash
Cost per serving ($):
7.95
Recipe:
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Add the cold butter and use your fingers or a pastry cutter to work the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse sand.
2. Gradually add the ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together in a ball. Shape the dough into a disk, wrap in plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
3. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
4. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef over medium-high heat, breaking it up with a wooden spoon. Add the onion, red bell pepper, and garlic and cook until the vegetables are softened, about 5 minutes.
5. Add the cumin, paprika, cayenne pepper, salt, and pepper to the skillet and stir to combine.
6. Add the olives and chopped eggs to the skillet and stir to combine. Remove from heat and let cool.
7. On a lightly floured surface, roll out the dough to 1/4 inch thickness. Cut out circles using a biscuit cutter or a large glass.
8. Place a spoonful of the meat mixture in the center of each circle. Fold the circle in half and crimp the edges together to seal. Brush the tops with egg wash.
9. Place the empanadas on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 20-25 minutes, until golden brown. Serve hot or at room temperature.
