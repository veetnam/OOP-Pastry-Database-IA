Name:
Turnovers
Information:
Turnovers are a popular pastry that can be made with shortcrust pastry. They are small, handheld pastries that are filled with a variety of sweet or savoury fillings and then folded over and baked until crispy and golden.
Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tsp salt
1 cup unsalted butter, chilled and cubed
4-6 tbsp ice water
1 cup cooked and shredded chicken
1/2 cup chopped cooked vegetables (such as carrots, peas, or broccoli)
1/2 cup shredded cheese
1 tsp dried thyme
Salt and pepper, to taste
1 egg, beaten
Cost per serving ($):
8.69
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt.
3. Add the cubed butter and use a pastry cutter or your fingers to work the butter into the flour until the mixture resembles coarse sand.
4. Add the ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together into a ball.
5. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface and knead gently until it comes together into a smooth ball.
6. Roll the dough out to a thickness of 1/4 inch (0.6 cm) and use a cookie cutter or a knife to cut out circles of dough (about 4 inches or 10 cm in diameter).
7. In a separate bowl, mix together the cooked chicken, vegetables, cheese, thyme, salt, and pepper.
8. Spoon a small amount of the filling onto one half of each dough circle.
9. Fold the other half of the dough over the filling and use a fork to crimp the edges of the turnover together.
10. Brush the turnovers with beaten egg and place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
11. Bake the turnovers for 20-25 minutes, or until they are golden brown and crispy.
12. Allow the turnovers to cool for a few minutes before serving.
