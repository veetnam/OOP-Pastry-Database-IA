Name:
Galettes
Information:
Galettes are a type of rustic, free-form tart that originated in France. 
They are made with a thin layer of shortcrust pastry, which is filled with a variety of savory or sweet ingredients, such as cheese, vegetables, fruit, or jam.
Galettes are typcailly servered as a light meal or dessert and can be enjoyed warm or at room temperature.
Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 teaspoon salt
1 cup unsalted butter, cold and cubed
1/4 to 1/2 cup ice water
2 cups of your choice of filling ingredients (e.g. cheese, vegetables, fruit)
Cost per serving ($):
10.50
Recipe:
1. To make the shortcrust pastry, combine the flour and salt in a large mixing bowl. Add the cold, cubed butter and use a pastry cutter or your hands to cut the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.2. 
2. Gradually add the ice water, 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring until the dough comes together into a ball.
3. Divide the dough in half and shape each half into a disk. Wrap the disks in plastic wrap and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
4. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
5. Roll out each disk of pastry dough on a floured surface until it is about 1/8 inch thick.
6. Place the rolled-out pastry dough onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
7. Add the filling ingredients to the center of each pastry round, leaving a border of about 1 1/2 inches around the edge.
8. Fold the edges of the pastry up and over the filling, pleating the dough as you go to create a rustic, free-form edge.
9. Brush the edges of the galettes with egg wash.
10. Bake the galettes in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the filling is hot and bubbly.
11. Serve the galettes warm or at room temperature, garnished with fresh herbs or a sprinkle of powdered sugar, if desired.
