Name:
Lemon bars
Information:
Lemon bars are a delicious and tangy dessert that make use of a buttery shortcrust pastry base.
Ingredients:
1 and 1/2 cups (180g) all-purpose flour
1/2 cup (115g) unsalted butter, at room temperature
1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
1/4 teaspoon salt
4 large eggs, at room temperature
1 and 1/2 cups (300g) granulated sugar
1/2 cup (60g) all-purpose flour
2/3 cup (160ml) freshly squeezed lemon juice
2 tablespoons lemon zest
Powdered sugar, for dusting
Cost per serving ($):
4.50
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease a 9x13 inch (23x33 cm) baking pan and line it with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on two sides.
2. To make the crust, cream the butter and sugar together in a large mixing bowl until light and fluffy. Add in the flour and salt, and mix until the mixture forms a crumbly dough.
3. Press the dough evenly into the bottom of the prepared pan. Use a fork to prick several holes in the crust. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until the crust is lightly golden.
4. While the crust is baking, prepare the filling. In a medium bowl, whisk the eggs and sugar together until smooth. Add in the flour and whisk until fully combined. Add in the lemon juice and zest, and whisk until smooth.
5. Once the crust is baked, remove it from the oven and pour the lemon filling over the top. Return the pan to the oven and bake for another 20-25 minutes, or until the filling is set and slightly golden on top.
6. Remove the pan from the oven and allow the lemon bars to cool completely in the pan. Once cooled, dust the top with powdered sugar.
7. Lift the bars out of the pan using the overhanging parchment paper and cut into squares. Serve and enjoy!
