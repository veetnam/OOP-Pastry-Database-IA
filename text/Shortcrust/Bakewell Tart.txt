Name:
Bakewell Tart
Information:
Bakewell tart is a classic British dessert consisting of a shortcrust pastry base, a layer of raspberry jam, and a frangipane filling made of ground almonds, butter, sugar, and eggs.
Ingredients:
225g shortcrust pastry
150g raspberry jam
125g unsalted butter, softened
125g caster sugar
2 large eggs, beaten
125g ground almonds
1 tsp almond extract
Flaked almonds, for decoration
Cost per serving ($):
5.77
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 190°C (375°F).
2. Roll out the shortcrust pastry on a floured surface and use it to line a 23cm (9-inch) tart tin. Trim the edges and prick the base with a fork.
3. Spread the raspberry jam evenly over the base of the pastry.
4. In a separate bowl, cream together the softened butter and caster sugar until light and fluffy.
5. Gradually add in the beaten eggs, then stir in the ground almonds and almond extract.
6. Spoon the frangipane mixture over the jam layer and smooth it out with a spatula.
7. Scatter some flaked almonds on top for decoration.
8. Bake the tart in the preheated oven for 35-40 minutes, or until golden brown and set.
9. Allow the tart to cool completely in the tin before removing and slicing into portions.
