Name:
Fruit tart
Information:
A fruit tart is a light and delicious dessert that is perfect for showcasing the flavors of fresh, seasonal fruit. The filo pastry crust is crispy and flaky, while the filling of sweetened cream cheese and fresh fruit is refreshing and flavorful.
Ingredients:
6-8 sheets filo pastry
1/2 cup butter, melted
8 oz cream cheese, softened
1/4 cup heavy cream
1/4 cup powdered sugar
Fresh fruit (e.g. berries, kiwi, mango)
Cost per serving ($):
13.25
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Lay out a sheet of filo pastry on a clean work surface and brush it lightly with melted butter. Layer another sheet of filo pastry on top and brush it with melted butter as well. Repeat this process until you have layered 6-8 sheets of filo pastry.
3. Cut the layered filo pastry into circles using a cookie cutter or a sharp knife.
4. Place the circles of filo pastry into a muffin tin, pressing them gently into the cups to create a tart shape.
5. Bake the filo pastry tarts in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until they are golden brown and crispy.
6. In a bowl, mix together the softened cream cheese, heavy cream, and powdered sugar until smooth and creamy.
7. Spoon the cream cheese mixture into the cooled filo pastry tarts, filling them about 2/3 full.
8. Arrange slices of fresh fruit on top of the cream cheese filling.
9. Serve the fruit tarts chilled, garnished with a sprinkle of powdered sugar or a drizzle of honey, if desired.
