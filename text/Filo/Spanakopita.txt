Name:
Spanakopita
Information:
Spanakopita is a classic Greek dish that is made with spinach, feta cheese, and filo pastry. The pastry is layered with a mixture of cooked spinach and feta cheese, and then baked until golden brown. 
This dish is perfect for a light lunch or as an appetizer for a party.

Ingredients:
1 package filo pastry sheets
1 pound frozen spinach, thawed and drained
8 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
2 eggs, beaten
1 onion, finely chopped
2 garlic cloves, minced
1/4 cup olive oil
Salt and pepper to taste
Cost per serving ($):
13.20
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and garlic and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the thawed spinach to the skillet and stir until heated through. Remove the skillet from the heat and allow the mixture to cool for a few minutes.
4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the beaten eggs and crumbled feta cheese. Add the spinach mixture to the egg and cheese mixture and stir to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
5. Lay out a sheet of filo pastry on a clean work surface and brush it lightly with olive oil. Layer another sheet of filo pastry on top and brush it with olive oil as well. Repeat this process until you have layered 5-6 sheets of filo pastry.
6. Spread the spinach and cheese mixture over the filo pastry, leaving a small border around the edges. Roll the pastry up tightly, tucking in the edges as you go, and place it seam-side down on a baking sheet.
7. Repeat the process with the remaining filo pastry sheets and spinach and cheese mixture, making 2-3 rolls.
8. Brush the tops of the pastry rolls with olive oil and bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.
9. Remove the Spanakopita from the oven and allow it to cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
