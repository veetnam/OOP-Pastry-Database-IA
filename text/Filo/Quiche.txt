Name:
Quiche
Information:
Quiche is a savory pie that originated in France and is popular worldwide. It is typically made with a pastry crust and filled with a mixture of eggs, milk or cream, cheese, and vegetables or meat. 
This recipe uses filo pastry for a lighter, flakier crust that is perfect for a delicious quiche.

Ingredients:
6-8 sheets filo pastry
4 eggs
1/2 cup milk or cream
1 cup grated cheese (cheddar, feta, or goat cheese work well)
1 cup chopped vegetables (spinach, mushrooms, onions, etc.)
2 tablespoons butter, melted
Salt and pepper to taste
Cost per serving ($):
12.30
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Grease a 9-inch pie dish with butter.
3. Lay out a sheet of filo pastry on a clean work surface and brush it lightly with melted butter. Layer another sheet of filo pastry on top and brush it with melted butter as well. Repeat this process until you have layered 6-8 sheets of filo pastry.
4. Carefully transfer the filo pastry to the greased pie dish and press it into the corners.
5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk or cream, and salt and pepper.
6. Stir in the grated cheese and chopped vegetables.
7. Pour the egg and vegetable mixture into the filo pastry crust.
8. Bake the quiche in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the filling is set and the filo pastry is golden brown and crispy.
9. Remove the quiche from the oven and allow it to cool for a few minutes before serving.
