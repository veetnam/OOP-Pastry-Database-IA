Name:
Börek
Information:
Börek is a popular savory pastry in Turkish cuisine, made with layers of filo pastry and a filling of feta cheese, spinach, and onions. The layers of filo pastry become crispy and golden brown when baked, while the filling is flavorful and satisfying.
Ingredients:
6-8 sheets filo pastry
1 cup crumbled feta cheese
1 cup chopped spinach
1/2 cup chopped onion
2 tablespoons butter, melted
Salt and pepper to taste
Cost per serving ($):
10.50
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a bowl, mix together the crumbled feta cheese, chopped spinach, chopped onion, salt, and pepper.
3. Lay out a sheet of filo pastry on a clean work surface and brush it lightly with melted butter. Layer another sheet of filo pastry on top and brush it with melted butter as well. Repeat this process until you have layered 6-8 sheets of filo pastry.
4. Cut the layered filo pastry into strips about 3 inches wide.
5. Place a spoonful of the feta cheese and spinach filling at one end of each strip of filo pastry.
6. Fold the filo pastry over the filling to form a triangle. Continue folding the filo pastry over the filling, maintaining the triangular shape, until you reach the end of the strip.
7. Brush the outside of the börek with melted butter.
8. Place the börek on a baking sheet and bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and crispy.
9. Serve the börek warm, as a delicious snack or appetizer.
