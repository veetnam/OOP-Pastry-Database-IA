Name:
Baklava
Information:
Baklava is a sweet pastry that originated in the Middle East and is popular throughout the Mediterranean region. It is made with layers of filo pastry, chopped nuts, and syrup or honey. 
The pastry is cut into diamond-shaped pieces and is a delicious dessert that is perfect for special occasions or as a sweet treat anytime.
Ingredients:
1 package filo pastry sheets
2 cups chopped nuts (walnuts or pistachios)
1 cup sugar
1 cup honey
1 cup butter, melted
1 teaspoon cinnamon
1 tablespoon lemon juice
Cost per serving ($):
17.21
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. In a large bowl, mix together the chopped nuts, cinnamon, and 1/4 cup of sugar. Set aside.
3. Brush the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish with melted butter.
4. Lay out a sheet of filo pastry on a clean work surface and brush it lightly with melted butter. Layer another sheet of filo pastry on top and brush it with melted butter as well. Repeat this process until you have layered 8-10 sheets of filo pastry.
5. Sprinkle 1/3 of the nut mixture over the top of the filo pastry.
6. Layer another 8-10 sheets of filo pastry on top of the nut mixture, brushing each sheet with melted butter as before.
7. Sprinkle another 1/3 of the nut mixture over the top of the filo pastry.
8. Layer another 8-10 sheets of filo pastry on top of the nut mixture, brushing each sheet with melted butter as before.
9. Sprinkle the remaining 1/3 of the nut mixture over the top of the filo pastry.
10. Layer the remaining filo pastry sheets on top of the nut mixture, brushing each sheet with melted butter as before.
11. Cut the baklava into diamond-shaped pieces using a sharp knife.
12. Bake the baklava in the preheated oven for 45-50 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.
13. While the baklava is baking, make the syrup by combining the remaining sugar, honey, and lemon juice in a small saucepan. Heat the mixture over medium heat until the sugar has dissolved.
14. When the baklava is done, remove it from the oven and pour the syrup over the top, making sure to cover all of the pastry. Allow the baklava to cool before serving.
