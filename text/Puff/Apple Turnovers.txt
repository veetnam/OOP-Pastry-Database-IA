Name:
Apple Turnovers
Information:
Apple turnovers are a classic pastry made with flaky puff pastry and a sweet apple filling. 
They are easy to make and are perfect for breakfast or dessert. They can be served warm or at room temperature and are a delicious treat for any occasion.
Ingredients:
2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
2 apples, peeled, cored, and chopped
2 tablespoons unsalted butter
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
1 egg, beaten
2 tablespoons coarse sugar, for sprinkling
Cost per serving ($):
5.32
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Roll out the puff pastry sheets on a lightly floured surface to a thickness of about 1/4 inch.
3. Cut each pastry sheet into 4 equal squares.
4. In a medium saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat.
5. Add the chopped apples, sugar, and cinnamon to the saucepan and stir to combine.
6. Cook the mixture for 5-10 minutes, or until the apples are tender and the mixture has thickened.
7. Remove the apple mixture from the heat and let it cool slightly.
8. Place a spoonful of the apple mixture in the center of each pastry square.
9. Fold each square in half diagonally to create a triangle.
10. Use a fork to press the edges of the pastry together to seal the turnovers.
11. Brush the beaten egg over the tops of the turnovers, and sprinkle the coarse sugar over them.
12. Place the turnovers onto the prepared baking sheet and bake for 15-20 minutes, or until they are golden brown and crispy.
13. Remove the turnovers from the oven and let them cool for a few minutes before serving.
14. Serve warm or at room temperature.
