Name:
Puff Pastry Cinnamon Rolls
Information:
Puff pastry cinnamon rolls are a flaky and delicious alternative to traditional cinnamon rolls. 
Made with store-bought puff pastry dough, these cinnamon rolls are easy to make and perfect for a quick breakfast or snack. 
They are filled with a cinnamon sugar mixture and drizzled with a sweet glaze.
Ingredients:
1/2 cup (1 stick or 113g) unsalted butter, softened
1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
1 cup (120g) powdered sugar
2 tablespoons milk
1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
Cost per serving ($):
7.65
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a small bowl, mix together the butter, sugar, and cinnamon to make the cinnamon filling.
3. Roll out the puff pastry on a lightly floured surface to a rectangle that is about 12x18 inches.
4. Spread the cinnamon filling evenly over the puff pastry, leaving a 1/2 inch border around the edges.
5. Starting from the long edge, roll the puff pastry tightly into a log.
6. Cut the log into 1 inch thick slices using a sharp knife or pastry cutter.
7. Place the cinnamon rolls onto the prepared baking sheet, spacing them about 1 inch apart.
8. Bake the cinnamon rolls for 15-20 minutes, or until they are golden brown and puffed up.
9. While the cinnamon rolls are baking, make the glaze by whisking together the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla extract in a small bowl.
10. Once the cinnamon rolls are done, let them cool for a few minutes before drizzling the glaze over the top.
11. Serve the puff pastry cinnamon rolls warm and enjoy!
