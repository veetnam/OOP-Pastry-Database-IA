Name:
Mille-feuille
Information:
Mille-feuille is a classic French pastry made up of layers of crispy puff pastry and rich vanilla pastry cream, all topped with a thin layer of icing sugar.
It's a beautiful and elegant dessert that's perfect for special occasions.
Ingredients:
2 cups whole milk
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup granulated sugar
4 egg yolks
1/4 cup cornstarch
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
2 sheets puff pastry, thawed
1 cup powdered sugar
2-3 tablespoons milk
Cost per serving ($):
4.89
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Roll out the puff pastry sheets on a lightly floured surface to a thickness of about 1/4 inch.
3. Cut each pastry sheet into 3 equal rectangles.
4. Place the rectangles onto the prepared baking sheet, and prick the surface with a fork to prevent them from puffing up too much during baking.
5. Bake the pastry rectangles for 10-15 minutes, or until they are golden brown and crispy.
6. Remove the pastry rectangles from the oven and let them cool completely.
7. While the pastry is cooling, make the pastry cream. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the milk, cream, sugar, egg yolks, cornstarch, and vanilla extract.
8. Heat the mixture over medium heat, whisking constantly, until it thickens and comes to a boil.
9. Remove the pastry cream from the heat and transfer it to a bowl. Cover the surface of the cream with plastic wrap to prevent a skin from forming, and let it cool to room temperature.
10. Once the pastry rectangles and pastry cream have cooled, assemble the mille-feuille. Place one pastry rectangle onto a serving plate and spread a layer of pastry cream over it.
11. Place another pastry rectangle on top of the cream, and spread another layer of cream over it.
12. Repeat the layering process until you have used up all of the pastry rectangles, finishing with a layer of cream.
13. Make the icing by whisking together the powdered sugar and milk in a small bowl until smooth.
14. Drizzle the icing over the top of the mille-feuille, and use a knife or spatula to spread it evenly.
15. Chill the mille-feuille in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour before serving.
16. Once it's chilled, slice the mille-feuille into pieces and serve.
