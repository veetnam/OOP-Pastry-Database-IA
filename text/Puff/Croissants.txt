Name:
Croissants
Information:
Croissants are a flaky, buttery, and crescent-shaped pastry that originated in France.
They are made from laminated dough, which is a dough that has been layered with butter and folded repeatedly to create multiple layers.
Croissants can be enjoyed plain or filled with a variety of sweet or savory ingredients, and they are typically served for breakfast or brunch.
Ingredients:
3 cups (375g) all-purpose flour
1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
2 1/4 teaspoons (7g) active dry yeast
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup (120ml) warm milk
1/4 cup (60ml) warm water
1 cup (2 sticks or 225g) unsalted butter, cold
1 egg, beaten
1 tablespoon water
Cost per serving ($):
5.75
Recipe:
1. In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, yeast, and salt.
2. Add the warm milk and warm water to the dry ingredients, and mix until a dough forms.
3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for 5-10 minutes until it becomes smooth and elastic.
4. Shape the dough into a ball, cover it with plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
5. Cut the cold butter into thin slices and place them on a sheet of plastic wrap to form a 6x8 inch rectangle. Cover the butter with another sheet of plastic wrap and use a rolling pin to flatten it into an even layer.
6. Roll the dough out on a floured surface to a rectangle that is 8x16 inches.
7. Place the flattened butter on top of the dough, leaving a 1 inch border around the edges.
8. Fold the dough over the butter to encase it completely, and press down the edges to seal.
9. Roll the dough out into a long rectangle that is 8x24 inches.
10. Fold the dough into thirds like a letter, making sure to brush off any excess flour in between each fold.
11. Rotate the dough 90 degrees, and repeat step 9 and 10 two more times, for a total of 3 folds.
12. Cover the dough with plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, or overnight.
13. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
14. Roll out the chilled dough into a large rectangle that is about 1/4 inch thick.
15. Use a sharp knife or pizza cutter to cut the dough into triangles.
16. Roll each triangle up, starting from the wide end and rolling towards the point.
17. Place the croissants on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, and brush them with the beaten egg mixed with 1 tablespoon water.
18. Bake the croissants for 15-20 minutes, or until they are golden brown and flaky.
20. Serve the croissants warm or at room temperature, plain or filled with your favorite sweet or savory fillings.
