Name:
Palmiers
Information:
Palmiers, also known as elephant ears or palm leaves, are a French pastry that are made from puff pastry dough and sugar. 
They are baked until crispy and caramelized, creating a sweet and flaky treat that's perfect for snacking.
Ingredients:
1 sheet of puff pastry dough, thawed
1/2 cup granulated sugar
Cost per serving ($):
1.65
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Sprinkle the granulated sugar over a clean work surface.
3. Unfold the thawed puff pastry sheet and place it onto the sugar. Use a rolling pin to gently roll the dough, pressing the sugar into the pastry on both sides.
4. Fold the long edges of the dough towards the center until they meet in the middle.
5. Fold the dough in half along the center fold, so that the two folded edges meet.
6. Slice the dough into 1/2 inch slices, and place them on the prepared baking sheet with the cut side facing up.
7. Bake the palmiers in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until they are golden brown and caramelized.
8. Remove the palmiers from the oven and transfer them to a wire rack to cool.
9. Once they have cooled, serve the palmiers and enjoy!
