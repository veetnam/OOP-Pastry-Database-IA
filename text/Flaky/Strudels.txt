Name:
Strudels
Information:
Strudels are a delicious pastry that originated in Austria and are now popular all over the world. 
Strudels are made with flaky pastry that is rolled very thin and then filled with sweet or savory ingredients. 
The filling is then rolled up into a spiral shape and baked until the pastry is crispy and golden.
 One of the most popular types of strudels is apple strudel, which features a sweet and tart apple filling flavored with cinnamon and sugar.
Ingredients:
2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup unsalted butter, cut into cubes
1/2 cup cold vegetable shortening, cut into cubes
6-8 tablespoons ice water
4 cups thinly sliced and peeled apples (about 4 medium-sized apples)
1/2 cup sugar
1 teaspoon cinnamon
1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
1/2 cup bread crumbs
1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
Powdered sugar, for dusting
Cost per serving ($):
9.54
Recipe:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour and salt. Add the butter and shortening and use a pastry cutter or your fingers to work the butter and shortening into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Add ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together into a ball.
4. Divide the dough in half and flatten each half into a disk. Wrap each disk in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
5. While the dough is chilling, prepare the apple filling. In a large bowl, toss the sliced apples with sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and bread crumbs until well coated.
6. On a lightly floured surface, roll out one of the dough disks into a very thin rectangle. Brush the dough with melted butter.
7. Sprinkle the bread crumbs over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border around the edges.
8. Add the apple filling to the dough, spreading it evenly over the bread crumbs.
9. Starting at one end, gently roll up the dough and filling into a spiral shape. Place the strudel on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
10. Brush the strudel with melted butter and bake for 30-40 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and crispy.
11. Let the strudel cool for at least 10 minutes before slicing. Dust with powdered sugar before serving.
