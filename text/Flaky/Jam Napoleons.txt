Name:
Jam Napoleons
Information:
Jam Napoleons with flaky pastry are a sweet and flaky dessert that consists of layers of flaky pastry sheets filled with jam and whipped cream. 
This dessert is easy to make and can be served at any occasion. The pastry is light and crisp, and the jam and whipped cream filling is sweet and creamy. 
These Napoleons are perfect for tea parties or as an afternoon snack.
Ingredients:
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/4 teaspoon salt
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cut into small pieces
3-4 tablespoons ice water
1/2 cup jam of your choice
1 cup heavy whipping cream
2 tablespoons powdered sugar
Cost per serving ($):
10.30
Recipe:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and sugar. Add the butter and use a pastry cutter or your fingers to work the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Add ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together into a ball.
4. Divide the dough into two portions and roll each portion into a thin sheet, about 1/8 inch thick. Cut the pastry sheets into rectangles, about 3 inches by 4 inches, and prick the surface with a fork.
5. Bake the pastry sheets for 10-15 minutes, or until golden brown. Remove from the oven and let cool.
6. In a medium-sized bowl, whip the heavy cream and powdered sugar until soft peaks form.
7. To assemble the Napoleons, place one pastry sheet on a plate, spread a layer of jam on top, and then add a layer of whipped cream. Repeat with another pastry sheet, jam, and whipped cream. Top with a third pastry sheet and dust with powdered sugar.
8. Repeat with the remaining pastry sheets and fillings to make additional Napoleons.
9. Chill the Napoleons in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving.
