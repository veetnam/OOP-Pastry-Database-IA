Name:
Custard Tartlets
Information:
Custard tartlets with flaky pastry are a classic dessert that is perfect for any occasion. 
These tartlets consist of a flaky pastry shell filled with a rich and creamy custard filling.
 They are small enough to serve as individual portions, making them ideal for parties or gatherings. 
The custard filling is usually flavored with vanilla, which gives it a sweet and aromatic taste.
Ingredients:
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/4 teaspoon salt
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cut into small pieces
3-4 tablespoons ice water
2 cups milk
6 egg yolks
1/2 cup granulated sugar
1 teaspoon vanilla
Cost per serving ($):
8.8
Recipe:
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and sugar. Add the butter and use a pastry cutter or your fingers to work the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
3. Add ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together into a ball.
4. Divide the dough into 12 portions and roll each portion into a ball. Press each ball into the bottom and up the sides of a greased muffin tin, making sure the pastry is evenly distributed.
5. Prick the bottoms of the pastry shells with a fork and bake for 10-15 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown. Remove from the oven and let cool.
6. While the pastry shells are cooling, prepare the custard filling. In a medium-sized saucepan, heat the milk over medium-high heat until it starts to steam.
7. In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg yolks and sugar until well combined. Gradually pour the hot milk into the egg mixture, whisking constantly.
8. Pour the mixture back into the saucepan and heat over medium heat, whisking constantly, until the custard thickens and coats the back of a spoon.
9. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract.
10. Pour the custard into the cooled pastry shells and bake for 10-15 minutes, or until the custard is set and golden brown on top.
11. Let the tartlets cool completely before removing them from the muffin tin. Serve the tartlets at room temperature.
