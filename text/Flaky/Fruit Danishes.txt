Name:
Fruit Danishes
Information:
Fruit danishes with flaky pastry are a delicious breakfast or brunch pastry that is perfect for entertaining or as a special treat. The flaky pastry dough is topped with a creamy and tangy filling and topped with fresh fruit. 
This dessert is easy to make and can be customized with your favorite fruits.
Ingredients:
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/4 teaspoon salt
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1/2 cup unsalted butter, cold and cut into small pieces
3-4 tablespoons ice water
4 ounces cream cheese, softened
1 tablespoon lemon juice
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1/4 cup powdered sugar
Fresh fruit of your choice, sliced
Cost per serving ($):
13.30
Recipe:
Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, and sugar. Add the butter and use a pastry cutter or your fingers to work the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
Add ice water, one tablespoon at a time, until the dough comes together into a ball.
Roll the dough out into a thin sheet, about 1/8 inch thick. Cut the pastry sheet into squares or rectangles, about 3 inches by 4 inches, and prick the surface with a fork.
In a medium-sized bowl, whisk together the cream cheese, lemon juice, vanilla extract, and powdered sugar until smooth.
Spread a spoonful of the cream cheese mixture onto each pastry square, leaving a small border around the edges.
Top the cream cheese mixture with fresh fruit slices, arranging them in a decorative pattern.
Bake the danishes for 15-20 minutes, or until the pastry is golden brown and the cream cheese filling is set.
Let the danishes cool for a few minutes before serving.
