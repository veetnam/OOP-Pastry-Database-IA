Name:
Profiteroles
Information:
Profiteroles are a classic French dessert made from choux pastry dough, which is light and airy. 
The dough is piped into small rounds and baked until puffed and golden brown. 
Once cooled, the rounds are filled with ice cream or whipped cream and drizzled with chocolate sauce or dusted with powdered sugar. 
Profiteroles are a popular dessert at dinner parties and special occasions.
Ingredients:
1 cup water
1/2 cup unsalted butter
1/4 teaspoon salt
1 cup all-purpose flour
4 large eggs
1 pint ice cream or whipped cream
1/2 cup heavy cream
1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
Cost per serving ($):
7.26
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium-sized saucepan, bring the water, butter, and salt to a boil.
3. Add the flour all at once and stir vigorously until the mixture forms a ball and pulls away from the sides of the pan.
4. Remove the pan from the heat and let the mixture cool for 5-10 minutes.
5. Add the eggs, one at a time, stirring vigorously after each addition until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
6. Using a piping bag or spoon, pipe or drop the dough onto the prepared baking sheet, making 12 mounds about 1-2 inches in diameter.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the profiteroles are puffed and golden brown.
8. Remove the profiteroles from the oven and let them cool completely on a wire rack.
9. Once the profiteroles are cooled, cut a small hole in the side of each one and fill with a scoop of ice cream or whipped cream.
10. To make the chocolate sauce, heat the heavy cream in a small saucepan over medium heat until it just comes to a boil.
11. Remove the saucepan from the heat and add the chocolate chips and vanilla extract. Stir until the chocolate chips are completely melted and the mixture is smooth.
12. Drizzle the chocolate sauce over the profiteroles or dust them with powdered sugar.
13. Serve the profiteroles immediately.
