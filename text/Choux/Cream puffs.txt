Name:
Cream puffs
Information:
Cream puffs are a classic French pastry made from choux pastry dough, which is light and airy. 
The dough is baked until puffed and golden brown, then filled with a sweet and creamy filling, usually pastry cream or whipped cream. 
Cream puffs can be served plain, dusted with powdered sugar, or drizzled with chocolate sauce.
Ingredients:
1 cup water
1/2 cup unsalted butter
1/4 teaspoon salt
1 cup all-purpose flour
4 large eggs
1 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup granulated sugar
1/4 cup cornstarch

Cost per serving ($):
8.66
Recipe:
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a medium-sized saucepan, bring the water, butter, and salt to a boil.
3. Add the flour all at once and stir vigorously until the mixture forms a ball and pulls away from the sides of the pan.
4. Remove the pan from the heat and let the mixture cool for 5-10 minutes.
5. Add the eggs, one at a time, stirring vigorously after each addition until the mixture is smooth and glossy.
6. Using a piping bag or spoon, pipe or drop the dough onto the prepared baking sheet, making 12 mounds about 2 inches in diameter.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the puffs are puffed and golden brown.
8. Remove the puffs from the oven and let them cool completely on a wire rack.
9. To make the pastry cream, in a medium-sized saucepan, whisk together the heavy cream, sugar, cornstarch, and vanilla extract.
10. Place the saucepan over medium heat and cook, whisking constantly, until the mixture thickens and comes to a boil.
11. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let the pastry cream cool completely.
12. Once the puffs and pastry cream are both cooled, use a piping bag or spoon to fill the puffs with the pastry cream.
13. Dust the cream puffs with powdered sugar, if desired, and serve immediately.
