Name:
Beignets
Information:
Beignets are a classic French doughnut that are popular in New Orleans cuisine. These deep-fried pastries are light and fluffy, with a crispy exterior and a soft, pillowy interior.
They are usually square or rectangular in shape and dusted with a generous amount of powdered sugar.
Ingredients:
2 cups all-purpose flour
1/4 cup granulated sugar
1 tablespoon baking powder
1/2 teaspoon salt
3/4 cup milk
1 large egg
Vegetable oil, for frying
Powdered sugar, for dusting
Cost per serving ($):
4.30
Recipe:
1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk and egg until well combined.
3. Add the milk mixture to the flour mixture and stir until a smooth dough forms.
4. On a lightly floured surface, knead the dough for 1-2 minutes until it becomes smooth.
5. Roll the dough out into a rectangle, about 1/4 inch thick.
6. Use a sharp knife or pizza cutter to cut the dough into 12 equal-sized squares or rectangles.
7. In a large, heavy-bottomed pot or Dutch oven, heat the vegetable oil to 350°F.
8. Once the oil is heated, carefully add a few of the beignets to the oil and fry until golden brown, flipping once, about 2-3 minutes per side.
9. Using a slotted spoon, remove the beignets from the oil and transfer them to a paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil.
10. Repeat with the remaining beignets, frying only a few at a time to prevent overcrowding in the pot.
11. Once all the beignets are fried and drained, dust them generously with powdered sugar.
12. Serve the beignets warm and enjoy!
