Name:
Classic Vanilla Eclairs
Information:
Classic vanilla eclairs are a French pastry consisting of a crispy choux pastry shell filled with a rich and creamy vanilla pastry cream and topped with a sweet glaze.
The choux pastry shell is made from a combination of water, butter, salt, flour, and eggs, which is piped onto a baking sheet and baked until golden and crispy. 
The vanilla pastry cream is made with whole milk, egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, vanilla extract, and butter, which is cooked on the stovetop until thickened and then chilled before being piped into the baked choux pastry shells.
 The sweet glaze is made from powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla extract and is used to top the filled eclairs, adding a sweet finish to the pastry. The result is a delightful dessert that is crispy, creamy, and sweet all at once.
Ingredients:
1 cup water
1/2 cup unsalted butter
1/4 teaspoon salt
1 cup all-purpose flour
4 large eggs
2 cups whole milk
1/2 cup granulated sugar
4 large egg yolks
1/4 cup cornstarch
2 teaspoons vanilla extract
2 tablespoons unsalted butter
1 cup powdered sugar
2 tablespoons milk
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
Cost per serving ($):
8.29
Recipe:
1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. To make the choux pastry, in a medium saucepan over medium heat, bring the water, butter, and salt to a boil. Add the flour all at once and stir vigorously until the mixture forms a smooth dough and pulls away from the sides of the pan.
3. Remove the pan from the heat and let it cool for 5 minutes. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, until the mixture is smooth and shiny.
4. Transfer the choux pastry to a piping bag fitted with a large round tip. Pipe the pastry onto the prepared baking sheet into 12 oblong shapes, each about 4 inches long and 1 inch wide.
5. Bake the eclairs for 20 to 25 minutes, until they are puffed, golden brown, and sound hollow when tapped. Remove the eclairs from the oven and let them cool completely on a wire rack.
6. To make the vanilla pastry cream, in a medium saucepan over medium heat, whisk together the milk, sugar, egg yolks, and cornstarch until the mixture thickens and boils, about 5 minutes. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract and butter until the mixture is smooth.
7. Transfer the pastry cream to a bowl and cover it with plastic wrap, pressing the plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the cream to prevent a skin from forming. Chill the pastry cream in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.
8. To assemble the eclairs, use a sharp knife to cut a slit along one side of each eclair. Fill a pastry bag fitted with a small round tip with the chilled pastry cream and pipe the cream into the eclairs through the slit.
9. To make the glaze, in a small bowl, whisk together the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla extract until the mixture is smooth.
10. Dip the top of each eclair into the glaze and let the glaze set for a few minutes before serving.
